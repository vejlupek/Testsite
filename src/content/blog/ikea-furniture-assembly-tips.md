## Before you start: preparation that saves hours

### Check the contents of the box

Before you screw anything together, lay out all the parts and check them against the list in the manual. Find out about a missing screw or panel now – not halfway through. IKEA replaces missing parts free of charge, but you need to know what's missing.

### Prepare your work area

Assembling on carpet or a wooden floor? Put the cardboard from the packaging underneath – scratches happen fast. A clean hard floor or an old blanket is ideal.

### Tools you'll need

IKEA includes Allen keys, but they make assembly slow. For faster work I recommend:
- **A cordless screwdriver** with bits and a sensitive clutch (set a low torque)
- **A rubber mallet** for dowels and connectors
- **A spirit level** – essential for wardrobes and shelves
- **A tape measure** – check dimensions before you fix anything to the wall

## The most common IKEA assembly mistakes

### 1. Doing the steps in the wrong order

Every manual has a logical sequence. Skip one step and you often have to take half the wardrobe apart again. Read the whole manual before you start.

### 2. Cam locks not fully tightened

A cam lock (the rotating connector) has to sit in the right position and be fully tightened. A loose cam lock = a wobbly wardrobe. Check each one after tightening.

### 3. Over-tightened screws in chipboard

Chipboard has limited strength. An over-tightened screw strips the thread, and fixing it is tricky. Tighten firmly, but stop as soon as you feel resistance.

### 4. Assembling without a spirit level

A wardrobe that isn't level wobbles, the doors won't close properly and the shelves tilt. Two minutes with a level saves hours of adjustments.

### 5. A sloppy back panel

The back panel (HDF board) stiffens the whole unit. Without it the wardrobe twists. Before you nail it on, make sure the frame is square – both diagonals must be the same length.

## How to assemble an IKEA PAX wardrobe

PAX is the wardrobe I assemble most often – and the one where people most often get stuck. The process in a nutshell:

1. **Measure your ceiling height.** The 236 cm PAX frame has to be tilted upright – in a room with a ceiling under roughly 240 cm you won't be able to stand it up. Then you need the lower 201 cm version.
2. **Assemble the frame lying on its back**, on a protective layer. Nail on the back panel while it's still lying down – after checking the diagonals.
3. **Stand it up with two people.** Raising the frame is the riskiest moment – on your own it's easy to break a corner or scratch the ceiling. No one to help? [Call me](/en/furniture-assembly-prague/) – I'll assemble a full PAX combination in a morning.
4. **Anchor it to the wall straight after standing it up**, before adding interior fittings and doors.
5. **Doors and hinges last.** The hinges have three adjustment screws (height, depth, side) – adjust the doors so the gaps are even all round.

One PAX frame with doors takes me about 45–60 minutes; a full 2–3 m combination takes 3–5 hours.

## How to anchor an IKEA wardrobe to the wall

Tall wardrobes and dressers (PAX, BILLY, MALM, HEMNES) must be anchored to the wall – it's not optional, it's a safety requirement, especially if you have children. A tipped-over dresser is one of the most common causes of injuries to small children at home.

1. **Find out what your wall is made of** – brick, concrete panel, drywall or hollow blocks.
2. **Choose the right wall plug.** IKEA doesn't include wall fixings – you need to buy plugs to suit your wall. See [How to Choose the Right Wall Plug](/en/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-wall-plug/).
3. **With drywall**, anchor into a stud or use special cavity wall plugs.
4. **Use the supplied anti-tip strap** or bracket and fix it with at least two screws – never just one.

## How to fix chipped edges

During transport or assembly, the laminate on an edge sometimes chips off. Solution: a touch-up marker or wax pencil in the furniture's colour – IKEA sells them for its own products.
